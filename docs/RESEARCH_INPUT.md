# Manual research input, M2

No provider API is enabled. Owner imports a research JSON bundle for a private manual listing from its Research scorecard button. All facts are operator-supplied and must be permitted to store. No URL is fetched. Empty template produces unknown scores. Fixtures can be previewed through the protected preview endpoint or CLI but cannot persist on a manual listing or enter real alerts.

Envelope:
```json
{"schemaVersion":1,"domain":"your-domain.com","dataset":"manual","subtypes":[],"metrics":{},"extensions":null,"history":[],"sales":[]}
```

Every known fact needs `value`, `source`, `url` (HTTPS evidence), `observedAt` (ISO, never future), `licenseRef`, `independenceKey`, `confidence` (0–1), and `evidenceClass` (`manual_review`, `third_party_reported`, `verified`, or fixture only in fixture mode). Omit unknown metrics or use null; they never gain points. A verified label is the owner's attestation to primary proof, never automated verification. Do not label a news report direct transaction proof.

Supported numerical judgments (0–100): meaning, recall, deliberateSpelling, communication, adaptability, keywordClarity, alternativeScarcity, providerCompetition, backlinkQuality, industryStrength, pronounceability, startupFit, typographicalAmbiguity and versatility. These are manually assessed interpretations, not measured facts or provider valuations. Independent source keys identify the underlying observation, not separate articles repeating it. Same-key components are grouped and capped at their largest weight, reducing correlated double count.

Raw metrics: population, income, homeValue, buyerCount, searchVolume, cpc, registrationAgeYears, observedDrops, legitimateUseYears, sameBusinessYears, syllableCount, referringDomains, backlinkCount, geographicReach. Keep `unit`, `currency`, `geography` and `period` as applicable. Financial transforms require independently sourced USD observations with explicit units: income USD/year, homeValue USD, cpc USD/click; unsupported/missing currencies stay missing, no invented FX. Counts and CPC are supporting research signals, never direct domain valuation. `commercialIntent` is informational, commercial_research, transactional, navigational_brand or ephemeral.

Fact shape (template only; null is deliberately unknown):
```json
{"value":null,"source":"Your permitted source","url":"https://example.com/replace-with-evidence","observedAt":"2026-11-16T08:00:00Z","licenseRef":"Replace with permission/reference","independenceKey":"Underlying observation ID","confidence":0,"evidenceClass":"manual_review","unit":"Specify units","geography":"Specify market","period":"Specify period"}
```

Extensions: provenance fields plus `tlds` (actual list or null), `usedByCompanies` (separate supported TLD-use list or null), `completeness` (complete/partial/unknown). Partial lists never become a global count/score. Complete exact lists use preliminary bands 0–2 / 3–4 / 5–9 / 10–19 / 20+. Registration does not prove same ownership, buyer interest, price or sale.

History: each snapshot has provenance plus `capturedAt` and `assessment` (unreviewed/legitimate/spam/phishing/malware/adult/gambling/redirect). CDX parser only creates unreviewed index records; it does not read or assess archived content. Repeated URL/capture observations dedupe. Positive cleanliness needs at least three reviewed legitimate snapshots spanning a year and the configured sampling coverage (default 3 of 5). This is heuristic sampling coverage, not proof every historical period was clean. Abuse or independently assessed low backlink quality can produce a low evidence score even under sparse sampling; either blocks promotion, removes public exposure and cancels pending alerts for that observation. New research always requires a fresh owner review. Private research scores are not yet inserted into Telegram payloads or public summaries; license-bound delivery fields remain a later integration gate. Legitimate history is not overall legal or security clearance; stability remains separate.

Sales: provenance plus domain, category, kind (sale/listing/estimate), amount (number or null), currency, soldDate (ISO or null), reportDate, confirmationClass (verified/third_party_reported/estimated/unknown), venue (text or null). Listings, estimates, unknown amounts/dates and unrelated structures are excluded from sold evidence. Transactions dedupe by domain/date/currency/amount, not number of news reports. At least two independent relevant sales are needed at default coverage. Third-party reports get reduced weight and remain reports. Local-service comps need the same service where detected; sale-age relevance decays after two years, and very old comps fall below the relevance gate. Prices are preserved, never extrapolated as a current candidate value.

Brandability supports all eight subtype labels: invented_coined, intentional_misspelling, blend, compound, commercial_prefix_suffix, acronym_initialism, dictionary_near_dictionary, geo_service_crossover. Auto labels are limited structure suggestions using a small original root vocabulary, not a dictionary or linguistic truth. Manual labels coexist. Acronyms bypass the consonant/pronunciation penalty and remove pronunciation/syllable preferences from their weight denominator; unusual spelling is eligible. Default 3–20 is soft; independent subtype boundaries/hyphen/digit preferences create review notes rather than universal exclusion. Compare explicit human communication evidence instead of a blanket radio gate.

`config/scoring-v1.json` defines separate weight sets, soft lengths and minimum coverage (default 60%). Configure via optional JSON in the editor; immutable config snapshots and hashed model versions retain reproducibility. Eligible does not mean available, safe or worth acquiring. Nine M2 dimensions implemented: brandability, geo_service, exact_match, cleanliness, stability, extension_adoption, comparable_sales, commercial_intent, replacement_cost. Company gap/end-user count await M3, trend awaits M4 and remain null. All twelve slots exported so missing modules remain visible.

Source URLs, source observation times, grouping keys, missing inputs, coverage, reasons, risks and next actions are retained per score. Rank within a chosen strategy with nulls last; no global sale-probability model. Protected API: POST /api/research stores owner manual bundles, GET /api/research/<listingId> returns own-tenant evidence, POST /api/research/preview does not save/send. The separate future /api/v1 remains disabled.

CLI preview after build: `node --experimental-strip-types src/cli.ts research-preview PATH.json`. No credentials needed. No automatic sales imports, trademark checks or source activation.
